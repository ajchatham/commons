Photorealistic aerial photograph from a drone about 500 feet up, looking south along a sunken interstate highway at the west edge of downtown Providence, Rhode Island, late afternoon in early October. Foreground: the highway in an open trench about 220 feet wide, six lanes of traffic plus ramps. A city street crosses the trench on a bridge. Directly behind that street, the entire width of the highway disappears into a long, low tunnel under a new flat deck built level with the city streets. The deck is exactly as wide as the trench and is divided into three blocks by city streets that cross it at street level. Block one, nearest the camera, is filled from edge to edge by a single cluster of six-story red brick apartment buildings with green roofs and shops on the ground floor; no park on this block. Block two, after the next cross street, and block three, after the one after that, are a long, narrow public park filling the whole deck: lawns, curving paths and rows of young trees in fall color. Only the deck changes: there is no open gap or light well in the deck, and no highway is visible between the first bridge and the far end. At the far end, about a third of a mile away, the highway comes back out of the tunnel into the open trench. Left (east) of the deck: downtown's older brick and granite office buildings and the 1928 Art Deco skyscraper with a stepped top. Right (west): the Federal Hill neighborhood of three-story wood-frame houses and brick buildings. Natural colors, documentary style, not a glossy architectural rendering.
