Photorealistic eye-level street photograph, early evening in late spring, on Atwells Avenue in Providence, Rhode Island, at the point where it crosses over a sunken interstate highway on its way from the Federal Hill neighborhood to downtown, looking east toward downtown's office towers in the distance. On the right side of the street: a new six-story red brick apartment building with tall ground-floor windows, a bakery and a small grocery with sidewalk tables, built on a deck that now covers the highway on that side of the street. On the left side of the street: a sidewalk, then a solid concrete parapet topped with a metal railing, and beyond it the highway is still open: a wide trench below street level with six lanes of cars and trucks running away from the camera, an on-ramp curving down into it, and on the far side of the trench a large shopping mall and parking garage. Young street trees, a brick sidewalk on the right, a crosswalk, people walking: an older couple, a man carrying groceries, two friends at a cafe table, a woman pushing a stroller, and one person pausing at the railing on the left to look down at the traffic. Warm evening light, 35mm lens, documentary style, natural colors, not a glossy architectural rendering. No readable text, no logos, no signs with words.
