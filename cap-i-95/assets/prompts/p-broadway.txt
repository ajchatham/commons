Photorealistic eye-level street photograph, early evening in late spring, on Broadway in Providence, Rhode Island, looking east toward downtown's office towers in the distance. This stretch of the street runs across a new deck built over a sunken interstate highway; the highway still runs in a tunnel under the deck, so none of it is visible. Everything is at one level, like any city street. On the left side of the street: a new six-story red brick apartment building with tall ground-floor windows, a bakery and a small grocery with sidewalk tables. On the right side of the street: the edge of a new public park with a lawn, young trees and a curving path, at the same level as the sidewalk. A two-lane street with a bike lane, a crosswalk, young street trees, brick sidewalks. People: an older couple walking, a man carrying groceries, two friends at a cafe table, a woman pushing a stroller, kids on the lawn. Warm evening light, 35mm lens, documentary style, natural colors, not a glossy architectural rendering. No readable text, no logos, no signs with words.
